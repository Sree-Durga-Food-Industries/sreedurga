FAQ

Questions, answered simply.

Common questions can be edited from the future admin panel.

Which payment methods are available?

UPI, debit/credit cards, bank transfer and cash on delivery are supported in the planned checkout flow. Payment credentials can be configured later.

Can I receive an invoice?

Yes. The system is designed to generate a downloadable PDF invoice automatically after an order is confirmed.

Where do you deliver?

Chennai delivery is the initial focus. Delivery zones, PIN codes and charges are configurable for future expansion.

Can I order for festivals or gifting?

Yes. The catalogue is designed to support combo packs, festival specials, gift boxes and bulk orders.
